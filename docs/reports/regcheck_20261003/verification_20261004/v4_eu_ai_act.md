Fact-check of bundle v4 (2026-10-04). EUR-Lex returned an empty 202 challenge page, so I read the legal texts from the Publications Office: the consolidated text 02024R1689-20260727, the original OJ act 32024R1689, the amending act 32026R1744 and its corrigendum. I read the Art. 50 guidelines PDF (C(2026) 5054 final, 51 pp.) with pdftotext. Every paragraph cited below is a numbered body paragraph, not a footnote: body para (21) is §2.4.2 R&D, while footnote (21) is about e-attestations.

research-exemption-is-narrow: PROBLEM (only in where the quote is sourced) — The quote is verbatim in Recital 25, which I checked in the original OJ text (32024R1689). But none of the cited sources carries it. The consolidated text has no recitals at all (0 hits for any Recital 25 sentence, no "Whereas"). The aia-art2 page only links "Related recitals: 25". Finck could not be opened (OUP returned 403), so I can't say whether it quotes the recital. Fix: cite the OJ original 32024R1689 for the recital.
  Everything else checks out:
  - Art. 2(6) wording is verbatim, and it says "put into service" with no "placed on the market".
  - Para. 21 is paraphrased faithfully: the exclusion covers "not only interactive AI systems, but also the outputs of generative AI systems … used in the context of scientific research", and the Art. 50 duties apply once a system is put into service or its outputs are used for other purposes.
  - The guidelines do not address a tool offered as a service to other researchers. I found no court ruling; the only AI Act reference to the CJEU (C-806/24, on Art. 86) was closed without an answer.
  - Caution, not an error: the worked example right after para. 21 describes researchers who "develop and place on the market or put into service" an interactive system and still fall under the exclusion. That sits awkwardly next to the body's "does not say placed on the market".

art-50-is-live: OK — Art. 50(1)-(5) as described matches the consolidated text, including 50(5) "clear and distinguishable … at the latest at the time of the first interaction or exposure". Para. 131 says text is published if "accessible by an indeterminate, fairly large number of unrelated, potential readers". It names a closed private group, "private, interpersonal correspondence (for professional purposes)" and "organisation-internal texts or communications" as not published.
  Caution on the title "in force now" (not on the body): the amendment added Art. 111(4). Generative systems placed on the market before 2026-08-02 have until 2026-12-02 to comply with Art. 50(2). Art. 50 itself applies from 2026-08-02 under Art. 113.

art-50-carve-outs: OK — The quote is verbatim in Art. 50(1) of the consolidated text. The paraphrases check out:
  - "assess and demonstrate": para. 42.
  - "average consumer" from EU consumer-protection law: para. 43.
  - "interpreted restrictively", and general awareness that AI exists "does not imply that they recognise them": para. 45.
  - The code-assistant example (professional developers only) and the example of systems used only by "properly trained health professionals" sit in the example box right after para. 45.
  - Review of substance by persons with relevant knowledge, with "Fact-checking … is a minimum requirement": para. 134.
  - Spell-checking, the mere existence of an editorial policy, automated review and cursory approval do not count: para. 135.
  - A substantive AI change after sign-off "will … cause the exception to become void": para. 136.
  - "Non-binding": para. 5.
  Minor location note: the academic-blog example (internal peer review, research centre holds editorial responsibility) is in the example box after para. 138, not inside paras. 133-136. The body cites no paragraph for it, so this is not an error.

penalties: OK
  - Art. 99(3): EUR 35 million / 7%, whichever is higher.
  - Art. 99(4)(g), which covers Art. 50: EUR 15 million / 3%, whichever is higher.
  - Art. 99(5): EUR 7.5 million / 1%, for "incorrect, incomplete or misleading information".
  - Art. 99(6): for SMEs, "whichever thereof is lower" across paragraphs 3, 4 and 5.
  - Art. 99(6a), added by the amendment: for SMCs, the lower figure for paragraphs 4 and 5 only.
  - The enforcement page (last update 24 August 2026) describes the framework and lists no enforcement decision.
